Vistaflo billing

Subscription and Billing Policy

These billing details apply to Vistaflo property website subscriptions operated by SHRI INFOWAVE PRIVATE LIMITED.

Customer clarity

Before payment, checkout shows plan amount, discount, paid credit, GST and final payable amount.

Plan management

Plans, prices, discounts, GST rate, trial settings, listing limits, staff limits and features are managed from super admin.

Payment flow

For purchase, renewal or upgrade, Vistaflo creates a Razorpay order, verifies payment signature, records billing details and activates or extends subscription.

Billing cycle

Customers can choose 1 month, 1 year or 2 years at checkout. Access starts or renews after successful payment verification.

Plan changes

Customers may upgrade or renew from dashboard. Last paid amount credit and GST are shown before Razorpay opens.

Invoices

Successful payments create invoice records with plan, amount, discount, GST, order/payment reference and payment status.

Access restriction

Expired, failed, disputed, past-due or suspended subscriptions may have restricted dashboard access until billing is resolved.
